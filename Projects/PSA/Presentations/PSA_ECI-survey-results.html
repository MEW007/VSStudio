Pillar 01 · Suitability

There are candidate projects. The team is new to ECI and every scope is early.

Low familiarity and an early scope point in the same direction: the first project needs a clear frame before any contractor is involved.

Everyone sits at 1 or 2. The room starts from a shared, low baseline, so the workshop has to build the vocabulary first: stages, gates, target price, open book.

Five answers, five different projects. Two name ECA projects, two point to business development and one names BHCT T4. The Manzanillo bid is a public tender, where local procurement rules may limit how early a contractor can be brought in.
For discussion
  1. Which one of these five projects would we take into Phase 1, and what makes it the best candidate?
  2. What would it take to move that scope from step 2 to step 3 before a contractor is involved?
  3. Can a public tender such as Manzanillo use ECI at all, or does it belong on a different track?
Pillar 02 · Readiness

Everyone expects the margin to come back through claims. A target price looks defensible.

The team knows how a lowest bid on an uncertain scope plays out. Most would find it reasonably easy to put a target price in front of leadership, if the costs can be verified.

Pillar 03 · Pathway to buy-in

A clear opt-out opens the door. There is no precedent to lean on yet.

Legal and Procurement would mainly want a way out before the construction contract. The proposal already builds that in as Gate 2. What is missing is a PSA example that shows it has worked before.

For discussion

  2. Which project used a different model and worked well? Can we use it as the internal precedent?
  3. What was the "something else" that Legal and Procurement would need to see?
Method

How to read the charts.

Method: with five answers every result is shown as a count. Q4 allowed two choices and Q6 and Q7 up to two, so their totals exceed five. The individual scores for Q1a, Q11 and Q5 are derived from the exported average, minimum, maximum and variance, which fix them exactly for five answers. Q11 was exported on a 0–4 scale and is shown here on the survey's 1–5 labels.
